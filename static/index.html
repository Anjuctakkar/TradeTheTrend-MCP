What is TradeTheTrend?

A dashboard that applies simple, fixed rules to past NSE prices and shows the results: charts, rule setups, sector momentum, rotation and more. It is for information and learning. It is not investment advice, and the rules can be wrong.

What does "No trade" mean?

The rules do not see a setup they like. That is a normal answer: the rules are picky on purpose, so most stocks get "No trade" on most days.

What is a LONG setup, and what are entry, stop and target?

A LONG setup is a plan to buy and hope the price rises. Entry is the price to buy at, stop is the safety line below it, and target is the goal above it. The setup is built from last night's close, so check the current price shown next to it.

What do the live, last trade and EOD badges mean?
  • live: the market is open and the price is fresh.
  • last trade: the market is closed; this is the last traded price.
  • EOD: no current price was available, so the last end-of-day close is shown.
Why does a setup say it no longer applies?

The current price is already below the stop or above the target, so the plan as written is out of date. Wait for the next day's rules output or study the stock again.

What is the difference between the two rule sets?

Trend and momentum checks averages, MACD and RSI. Support and resistance looks for bounces off price floors and breakouts through price ceilings. Each has its own entry, stop and target, and the stock card notes whether they agree.

What does 1D mean in the Sectors tab?

The newest traded price (LTP) compared with yesterday's close. When the market is closed it is the last trade against the previous close.

Why does loading sectors take so long, and why is a stock missing?

The first load fetches many stocks from NSE. Load one sector at a time to be quicker. Sector lists are short, hand-picked baskets, so some stocks are missing. Add your own in sector_map.json (the Sectors tab shows where it goes). A stock that could not load is listed under Not included below the sector chart.

What are sector rotation and early movers?

Rotation shows each sector's strength against the other sectors you loaded. A sector that is still behind but speeding up is Improving and flagged as an early mover. Rotations often fail, so confirm with the stocks themselves.

Are "Stocks to review" recommendations?

No. They are a mechanical shortlist of stocks in strong sectors with a positive month and LONG setups, with reasons and cautions. Review each one yourself, and check its current price and costs.

Where is my Evaluation list kept?

On your own computer, in your browser, not on the server. A browser can clear its storage, so use Create a file or Download a copy in the Evaluation tab to keep a copy.

Why does the Assistant say it is unavailable?

It needs an Anthropic API key with credit, set by an administrator in Settings. For sector questions, open the Sectors tab and click Load sectors first.

How do I filter or sort a table?

Type in the small box under a column name. Numbers accept >5, <=-2 and 3..8; put a comma between choices (TCS, INFY); start with ! to leave something out. Click a column name to sort and Clear filters to start over.

The page looks old or something is missing

Press Ctrl+F5 to refresh fully. The build stamp next to the title and /health show which version the server is running. If the server was not restarted after an update, new features will not appear.

Settings says the Bhavcopy or CM server returned 404 or is not working

The message now shows what NSE's server said. If it says Session not found, NSE's server did not recognise the session the app had just opened (this can happen when NSE runs several servers behind one address, or restarts one). The app now retries with a new session automatically. If it keeps failing, wait a few minutes and run Test NSE connection again, and try from your own computer to compare. Recently loaded data may still work from the cache for a while.

Is any of this investment advice?

No. Everything here is information from simple rules applied to past prices. Decisions are yours; consider speaking to a SEBI-registered adviser, and never use money you cannot afford to lose.

Sector momentum

Each sector is an equal-weight index of its large stocks, built from adjusted prices. It is a proxy, not an official NSE sector index. Use it to see where momentum is, then analyse the stocks inside.
Sectors follow NSE's 12 macro sectors and their industry groups. Loading all sectors fetches about 290 stocks and can take 30 minutes or more; pick one sector in Load to fetch 4 to 56 stocks (the largest sector takes several minutes). Loaded sectors are reused for 15 minutes, and switching the Stocks view needs no reload. Edit the stock lists with a sector_map.json file in your data folder.
Click Load sectors to rank the sectors by momentum.

Funding rules (MTF, interest, charges)

Used by Portfolio analysis when you choose "Own capital + MTF", and for charges and net P/L. Saved to your account, so they follow you to any device. Brokerage, DP and pledge values start as placeholders: enter your broker's.
One stock per line: SYMBOL 3x (leverage), SYMBOL 25% (own margin) or SYMBOL none (not on MTF). Stocks not listed use the default leverage. Leverage is limited to 1x to 4x (minimum own margin 25%). Lines starting with # are notes.
Charges
GST applies to brokerage, exchange, SEBI, DP and pledge fees, not to STT or stamp duty. Statutory rates are pre-filled from current NSE delivery rates and can change.

NSE connection

Checks that both NSE MCP servers answer and how fast. If a server is slow or down, analysis, backtests and the assistant's lookups will time out, whatever is set up on this side.
The app waits up to 60 seconds per request and tries twice. Change this with NSE_READ_TIMEOUT, NSE_RETRIES and NSE_MAX_INFLIGHT in your .env file, then restart.

Discover NSE tools

Lists every tool the two NSE servers offer. Tools whose name or description mentions sectors, industries, indices, constituents or classification are highlighted at the top, since those could supply sector lists. Administrators only.

Getting a key

  • Create a key in the Anthropic Console, API keys, and copy it when it is shown. It isn't shown again.
  • The key needs credit to work. Add some under Plans & Billing. "Credit balance is too low" means there is none left.
  • If a link has moved, look for API keys or Plans & Billing in the Console's Settings menu.
  • This page only works from the computer running the dashboard. Don't share the key or put it in website code.
